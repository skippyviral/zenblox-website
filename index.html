FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers for Engineering & Business Leads

What automation and cloud platforms do you build on?

We specialize in Make (Integromat), n8n, Zapier, Google Cloud (Vertex AI), and Microsoft Power Platform. Our engineers are certified across all major automation ecosystems and can work with your existing stack or recommend the best fit for your use case.

How fast can we expect our first automated agent in production?

Most targeted builds go live within 2 weeks. Complex enterprise integrations with multiple systems typically take 4-6 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during our discovery call based on your specific requirements.

Do we own the automation workflows and code?

Yes, 100%. All workflows, custom code, and configurations are deployed directly into your cloud tenants. You own everything we build. We provide full documentation and knowledge transfer so your team can maintain and extend the automations independently.

How do you handle LLM hallucinations and data confidentiality?

We implement retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with verified source attribution to minimize hallucinations. For confidentiality, we use enterprise LLM deployments (Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI) that don't train on your data, combined with human-in-the-loop checkpoints for critical decisions.
